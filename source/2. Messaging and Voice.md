Hojil helps enterprises in Mongolia improve efficiency with AI and custom software. We develop AI systems that help teams process documents, handle requests, and find information in the software they use every day.

## What we can show

- **AI development.** `hojil-tts` is an experimental Khalkha Mongolian text-to-speech project. Hojil also plans to publish open-source evaluations of how language models perform on tasks in Mongolian.
- **Client work.** We built Golomt Insights for Golomt Bank, Gangan for Urangan Corporation, and Gerelty Today. All three are websites with content management systems. Use them as examples of software delivery and describe each project's scope as documented.

## How we sound

- **Start with the work.** Name what the team does and where time goes. Say “pull the key details from incoming applications” when that describes the task.
- **Be specific about results.** Name what we can measure, such as time per request, processing errors, or how often staff have to redo a step. Quote improvements when we have measured them.
- **Use technical terms when they help the reader.** An operations lead needs to know what staff will see and do. A technology lead may need to know which systems the tool will connect to and how data moves between them.

## Examples by situation

**First conversation**

> Which task takes your team the most time each week? We can walk through how it works today and see whether part of it is worth automating.

**Request intake**

> Let's walk through what happens after a request arrives. Where do staff spend the most time collecting documents, checking information, or entering it into another system?

**Pilot proposal**

> We'll measure how long staff spend processing a request today. During the pilot, the AI system will extract key details from incoming documents and prepare a draft record for staff to review. We'll compare handling time and how often staff need to correct the record.

## Language for publishing

Proposed default for enterprise sales and marketing in Mongolia is Mongolian. Use English where partners, developers, or open-source contributors need it. Edit client-facing Mongolian copy against the intended meaning and how buyers talk about the work.
